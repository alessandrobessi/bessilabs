Thinking in Groove is an interactive, piano-score-based book about how musicians understand and design bass lines. The left hand is the bass; the right hand makes its harmonic and rhythmic context audible.

Just as [Thinking in Layers](/notes/thinking-in-layers) teaches harmony through superimposed musical layers, Thinking in Groove organizes each decision as Role → Motion → Groove → Interaction → Design — forty chapters and over 160 canonical grand-staff examples, each with independent full/bass/harmony playback.
